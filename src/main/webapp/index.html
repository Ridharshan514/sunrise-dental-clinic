Staff Onboarding & Help Guide

Step-by-step instructions for receptionists and clinic staff.

1. Authentication & Security

Staff members must sign in with their given username and password. Sessions are securely maintained per clinic security policies.

2. Registering Appointments

Enter patient full name, residential address, and a 10-digit Sri Lankan phone starting with 07. Select the dentist and procedure. The system prevents double-booking the same doctor at the same time.

3. Searching Records

Search via the unique APP-XXXX number to instantly retrieve patient contact, assigned dentist, and procedure details.

4. Invoicing & Billing

Enter the appointment number in the Billing section to calculate the total fee (Consultation Fee + Procedure Base Rate) and generate an official printable clinic receipt.
